What Makes Lumina Studio Unique

100% Local Sovereignty

All image transformations, matrix convolutions, and uploads remain strictly inside your local machine. No external cloud endpoints receive your sensitive images.

Smart AI GrabCut Cutouts

Advanced foreground segmentation with adaptive color sampling. Accurately preserves dark shirts, hair, torso, and chairs without hollowing out or face-only stripping.

22+ Vision Presets & Adjustments

Planckian color warmth (Golden Hour vs. Cyberpunk), CLAHE adaptive contrast, bilateral skin smoothing, 3D relief emboss, cartoonification, and False-Color palettes.

Dimension-Matched Comparator

Before & After Split Slider with hardware-accelerated CSS clip-path and auto dimension-matching against Previous Step or Original Photo to prevent aspect-ratio warping.

Vector SVG Tracing

Converts discrete raster bitmaps into scalable vector graphics (SVG) paths using K-Means color quantization and Suzuki-Abe topological contour following.

Dual Creative Studio Themes

Fluidly switch between High-Contrast Dark Studio mode (designed for photography and color grading) and Crisp Light mode with colorful custom slider tracks.
