Background Removal & Format Converters

AI Background Remover

AI GrabCut • Transparent PNG

Iterative Gaussian GrabCut foreground segmentation that isolates the central subject, generates an anti-aliased alpha mask, and outputs a 4-channel transparent PNG.

Example Prompts
remove background
remove bg
isolate subject cutout

Manual Background Eraser

Interactive Brush • Full Resolution

Pixel-precise canvas brush with adjustable radius (5px to 150px), circular cursor preview, multi-level undo stack, and real-time transparency checkerboard feedback.

How to Use
Click 'Manual Eraser' in Geometry/Presets
Brush over background pixels & click 'Save Cutout'

Color Key Background Remover

Chroma / Color Key

Isolates solid colored studio backdrops (white product shots, green screens, black darkroom backgrounds) and converts matching pixels to transparent alpha.

Example Prompts
remove white background
remove black background
remove green background

SVG Vector Converter

Vectorization • Scalable SVG

Transforms raster images into scalable, resolution-independent vector graphics via K-means color quantization, bilateral smoothing, and polygonal contour approximation (cv2.approxPolyDP).

Example Prompts
vectorize to svg
convert to svg 8 colors
Export Modal > SVG (Vector)

PNG Format Converter

Lossless • Transparency Support

Converts bitmap images into lossless PNG format with 4-channel BGRA alpha transparency support for cutouts, graphics, and web assets.

Example Prompts
convert to png
make png

JPG / JPEG Converter

Compressed • Web Standard

Flattens transparent alpha channels over a clean white background and compresses into web-optimized standard JPEG images with customizable quality.

Example Prompts
convert to jpg
make jpg

Exposure & Lighting

Brightness

Value (-100 to 100)

Offsets pixel luminance uniformly across all color channels using cv2.convertScaleAbs.

Example Prompts
brighten by 30
make the image darker

Contrast

Factor (0.5 to 2.5)

Expands or compresses tonal distance between shadows and highlights via scalar multiplication.

Example Prompts
increase contrast by 25%
decrease contrast factor 0.8

CLAHE Dynamic Contrast

New • LAB Equalization

Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization applied to the L-channel of the LAB color space, revealing hidden shadow details.

Example Prompts
enhance lighting with clahe
adaptive histogram contrast

Gamma Correction

New • LUT Power-Law

Applies non-linear power-law transform to recover dark shadows and balance midtones without blowing out highlights.

Example Prompts
lift shadows with gamma 1.5
gamma correction 1.4
